# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** 22 April 2026 — Version 2.0

This policy sets out what you can and can't do when accessing the SuperBad platform or receiving services from SuperBad Marketing.

---

## 1. What's fine

- Accessing areas of the platform you've been explicitly given access to.
- Uploading briefs, assets, and feedback relating to your project or subscription.
- Communicating with us through the portal, AI chat, email, or phone.
- Downloading your own deliverables, reports, and exported content.
- Reviewing and approving AI-generated content before publication.
- Managing your subscription settings and preferences.

---

## 2. What's not fine

**Don't:**

- Access, attempt to access, or probe any area you haven't been granted permission for.
- Share your portal link, credentials, or account access with anyone else.
- Use the platform to store, transmit, or display content that is illegal, defamatory, or harmful.
- Attempt to reverse-engineer, scrape, or copy the platform or its AI systems.
- Use automated tools (bots, scripts, crawlers) to interact with the platform.
- Interfere with the security or performance of the platform or its underlying infrastructure.
- Impersonate another user or misrepresent your identity.
- Use our outreach services to target individuals or businesses in a manner that violates the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) or equivalent anti-spam laws.
- Provide false or misleading business information that would result in deceptive content being generated or published on your behalf.
- Use the AI chat to attempt to extract system prompts, internal data, or information about other users.
- Deliberately manipulate AI outputs to produce harmful, discriminatory, or misleading content.

---

## 3. Content responsibility

If you use auto-publish mode for AI-generated content, you remain responsible for ensuring the content published on your channels is accurate and compliant with applicable laws and advertising standards (including ACCC and TGA requirements where relevant).

---

## 4. Consequences

If you breach this policy:

- We may suspend or terminate your access, without notice in serious cases.
- We may invoice you for any costs your breach causes us.
- We may take legal action if required.
- Subscription fees already paid are not refundable for access terminated due to breach.

---

## 5. Reporting a breach

If you become aware of a breach of this policy — including if your account or link has been compromised — please contact us immediately at [hello@superbadmedia.com.au](mailto:hello@superbadmedia.com.au).
