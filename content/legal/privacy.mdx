# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 22 April 2026 — Version 2.0

SuperBad Media ABN 92 686 683 894, trading as SuperBad Marketing ("SuperBad", "we", "us") is committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what your rights are.

We comply with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

---

## 1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to:

- Prospective and current clients of SuperBad Marketing.
- Subscribers to SuperBad products (BlackBox, Automated Outreach, Content Engine, Living Website, Review Responses).
- Users of the SuperBad platform (client portal, subscriber portal, and admin surfaces).
- Visitors to our website or landing pages.
- Anyone who contacts us by email, phone, or social media.
- Prospects contacted through our outreach services.

---

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | Name, business name, ABN, job title |
| **Contact** | Email address, phone number, physical address |
| **Business information** | Brand DNA profile, ICP definitions, service offerings, target audience, industry, keywords, business hours, locations |

We collect only what we need to deliver and improve our services.

---

## 3. How we use your information

- **Service delivery** — content generation, outreach execution, website hosting, ad management, shoot coordination, deliverables, invoicing, portal access.
- **AI processing** — your Brand DNA, business information, and content preferences are used as context for AI-generated content, outreach emails, review responses, and chat interactions. This processing is essential to delivering personalised services.
- **Communication** — project updates, billing notices, service notifications, weekly digests.
- **Platform improvement** — error monitoring, usage patterns, service quality analysis. No third-party ad tracking on our platform.
- **Legal compliance** — record-keeping as required by Australian tax law (ATO 7-year retention), Spam Act compliance records for outreach services.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising on behalf of anyone other than you (the client or subscriber).

---

## 5. AI and automated decision-making

We use artificial intelligence extensively in our services:

- **Content generation** — blog posts, social content, website copy, and review responses are generated by AI using your Brand DNA and business context.
- **Outreach** — prospect identification, ICP scoring, email personalisation, and reply classification are AI-driven.
- **Lead management** — AI provides first responses to inbound enquiries on your behalf.
- **Chat** — the subscriber portal AI chat reads your account data to answer questions and take actions.

AI outputs are generated by Anthropic's models. Your data is processed under Anthropic's commercial API terms, which prohibit using your data to train their models.

No fully automated decisions with legal or significant effects are made about individuals without human oversight.

---

## 6. Storage and security

Your data is stored on servers located in Australia (primary) and Cloudflare's nearest data centres (CDN and backups). We use AES-256-GCM encryption for sensitive credentials. Access to the platform requires authentication.

We retain records for 7 years as required by ATO guidelines. Portal access and non-active data may be archived earlier.

Subscriber data (Brand DNA, content, performance metrics) is retained for 30 days after subscription cancellation, then deleted unless a longer period is required by law.

## 8. Your rights (Australian Privacy Principles)

Under the APPs you have the right to:

- **Access** personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or out-of-date information.
- **Request deletion** (subject to our legal retention obligations).
- **Complain** to us or to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you believe we've mishandled your data.

For outreach subscribers: you may request a copy of prospect data associated with your account. Prospects contacted through our service may request removal from future outreach — we honour these requests promptly.
