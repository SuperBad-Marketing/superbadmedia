Template system

Each style above is an HTML/CSS template at /templates/social/. The Content Engine fills them with blog content + Brand DNA visual tokens (colours, type, spacing, mood), then Puppeteer renders to production-ready PNG. Claude selects the template, format, and platform per draft. Subscribers get the same system, pulling their own Brand DNA — not SuperBad's.

Layers: film grain overlay · geometric shapes (arcs, circles, triangles, ruled lines) · bleed typography · offset colour blocks · ghost type outlines · gradient washes · dot grids.
