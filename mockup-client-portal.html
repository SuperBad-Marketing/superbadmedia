Brand DNA

You run a firm that talks like a handshake — direct, calm, a little formal until it isn't. There's a restlessness under the polish. You're not chasing trends, but you're watching who is.
Portrait

Mercer Advisory presents like old money that's spent a decade in startups. The brand leans into restraint — muted colours, considered typography, negative space — but it's the restraint of someone who's looked at the loud option and chosen otherwise. Not minimalist for minimalism's sake. Selective.

Your communication style favours precision over warmth, but the warmth is there — it just arrives on its own terms. You write like someone who edits. Clients of yours probably feel handled, in the best sense. Organised. Anticipated. Nobody's chasing invoices.

The creative instinct is editorial. You're drawn to work that earns its confidence quietly — a brand that doesn't need to explain itself, a photograph where the crop does half the talking. You'd rather under-promise than over-style.
