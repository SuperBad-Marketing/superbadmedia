# Social Media Strategy

Frameworks for analysing performance, planning content, and growing an audience on Instagram (extensible to other platforms later).

## Content Pillars

Every account needs 3-5 content pillars — recurring themes that the audience expects. For a marketing company like SuperBad, strong pillars are:

1. **Portfolio / work showcase** — the actual work. Before/after transformations, behind-the-scenes process, final deliverables. This is the proof.
2. **Industry observation** — dry takes on marketing trends, what other brands get wrong, what works and why. This is the authority.
3. **Anti-motivation / real talk** — the grind reframed as proof of progress. Not hustle porn — the opposite. Honest about the work without romanticising it.
4. **Client stories** — results, testimonials, transformations. Social proof that converts.
5. **Personal / founder** — Andy as a person. Builds connection. Sparse — maybe 1 in 10 posts.

### Pillar Mix
- 40% portfolio/showcase
- 25% observation/authority
- 15% anti-motivation
- 15% client stories / social proof
- 5% personal

## Posting Cadence

### Frequency
- **Minimum viable:** 3 posts/week. Below this, the algorithm deprioritises.
- **Sweet spot:** 4-5 posts/week. Enough to stay visible without burning out content quality.
- **Max before diminishing returns:** 7/week (daily). Beyond this, posts cannibalise each other's reach.

### Timing
- Instagram engagement peaks: Tuesday–Thursday, 6-9am and 6-9pm local time.
- Avoid weekends for business content (engagement drops 20-40%).
- Post when your audience is active, not when you're free. Check Instagram Insights → Audience → Most Active Times.
- Consistency matters more than optimality. Same time every day > perfect time inconsistently.

### Content Type Rotation
- Carousels: 2-3x/week (highest save rate, best for authority content)
- Single images: 1-2x/week (portfolio, quotes)
- Reels: 1-2x/week (highest reach, best for discovery)
- Stories: daily (keeps you at top of feed, low-effort engagement)

## Algorithm Signals (2025-2026)

Instagram ranks content by predicted engagement, influenced by:

### Primary Signals
1. **Save rate** — the strongest signal. Saves indicate "I want to come back to this." Educational content, actionable tips, and reference-worthy posts win here. Carousels dominate saves.
2. **Share rate** — the reach multiplier. Shares = the post appeared in DMs or Stories. Relatable observations and spicy takes get shared.
3. **Time on post** — how long someone looks. Carousels with 5+ slides, dense captions, and detailed images keep attention.
4. **Comments** — weighted by depth. "Love this!" matters less than a 3-sentence reply. Conversation-starting captions drive meaningful comments.

### Secondary Signals
5. **Profile visits after viewing** — indicates the post made someone curious about you.
6. **Follow rate from post** — new followers attributed to a specific post.
7. **Completion rate** — for carousels: how many slides were swiped through. For reels: watch-through rate.

### What the Algorithm Penalises
- Engagement bait ("tag 3 friends!", "comment YES if you agree")
- Watermarked reposts from other platforms (especially TikTok)
- Low-resolution images
- Excessive hashtags (the spam signal)
- Posting and immediately deleting/reposting
- Rapid follow/unfollow behaviour

## Growth Mechanics

### Organic Growth Levers
1. **Carousel depth** — 7-10 slide carousels with genuine value get saved and shared. Each save extends the post's algorithmic life by hours.
2. **First-frame hook** — the scroll-stop. 0.3 seconds to earn attention. High contrast, unexpected image, or a statement that creates a knowledge gap.
3. **Caption structure** — open with an observation (not a question), deliver insight in the middle, close with a thought (not a CTA). Let the value speak.
4. **Collaborative posts** — co-authoring with clients or partners doubles the audience exposure. Use Instagram's native collab feature.
5. **Consistent visual identity** — a recognisable grid makes profile visitors convert to followers. They should know it's your post before reading the caption.
6. **Reply to every comment** — within the first hour if possible. Each reply counts as additional engagement and keeps the conversation going.

### Conversion: Followers → Leads
- Bio link to a high-converting landing page (not the homepage — a specific offer or portfolio page).
- DM automation for specific keywords (e.g., "PORTFOLIO" triggers a pre-written response with a link).
- Story CTAs ("Link in bio" sticker) for time-sensitive offers.
- Don't sell in every post. The ratio should be 80% value / 20% commercial intent — and the 20% should feel like a natural extension of the value, not a pivot.

## Performance Analysis Frameworks

### Post-Level Scoring
Rate each post on a composite score:
```
engagement_score = (saves × 4 + shares × 3 + comments × 2 + likes × 1) / reach × 100
```
Weight saves and shares highest — they're the algorithm's strongest signals.

### Cohort Analysis
Compare posts by:
- Content type (carousel vs single vs reel)
- Content pillar (portfolio vs observation vs anti-motivation)
- Day of week
- Time of day
- Caption length (short < 100 chars, medium 100-300, long 300+)

Look for patterns: "Carousels posted Tuesday morning with long captions outperform everything else 2:1."

### Growth Trajectory
Track weekly:
- Net follower growth (follows - unfollows)
- Follower growth rate (% change week-over-week)
- Engagement rate trend (is it rising with follower count, or diluting?)
- Reach per post average (is the algorithm showing your content to more people?)

A healthy account: follower growth rate > 1%/week, engagement rate > 3%, reach per post > 30% of followers.

### Red Flags
- Follower growth stalls but engagement is steady → algorithm isn't distributing to non-followers. Need more shareable/discoverable content.
- High reach but low engagement → content is being shown but not resonating. Quality issue.
- High engagement but low follower conversion → profile/bio needs work. People like the post but don't follow.
- Engagement rate dropping as followers grow → audience dilution. Common and somewhat inevitable, but slow the decline with quality.

## Content Ideas Engine

When generating content recommendations from metrics data, use this framework:

1. **Double down on winners** — identify the top 20% of posts by engagement score. What do they have in common? Recommend more of that.
2. **Fix the bottom** — the worst-performing 20%. What went wrong? Wrong content type? Bad timing? Off-brand?
3. **Experiment budget** — 20% of posts should try something new. New content type, new pillar, new format. Track results separately.
4. **Seasonal/timely hooks** — industry events, holidays, trending topics. But only if there's a genuine SuperBad take — never post just because it's trending.
5. **Series potential** — if a post performs well, can it become a recurring series? "Things marketers say" → weekly series. Audiences love predictability within novelty.
