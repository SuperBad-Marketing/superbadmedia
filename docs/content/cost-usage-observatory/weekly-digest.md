# Cost & Usage Observatory — Weekly Digest Email

Content source for CUO spec §7 prompt #3 (`draft-weekly-digest.ts`). Full house voice — this is a Sunday-evening read where voice leads.

---

## Subject-line pool (~5 variants, sprinkle-claimed)

Posture-adaptive: calm if MTD is green, observant if amber, direct if red.

### Green (MTD below first threshold)

1. "the observatory report — all quiet"
2. "your week in numbers — nothing caught fire"
3. "sunday cost check — the bands held"

### Amber (MTD between thresholds or projection crossing)

4. "the observatory report — a few things to note"
5. "weekly costs — drifting, not alarming"

### Red (MTD above highest threshold or severe anomaly open)

6. "the observatory report — needs your attention"
7. "weekly costs — above the line you drew"

---

## Opener pool (~6 variants)

First paragraph of the digest. Sets tone for the whole email.

1. "Here's what the platform cost this week. Most of it was expected."
2. "Weekly numbers. Nothing dramatic unless the section headings suggest otherwise."
3. "The observatory ran its sweep. Here's the summary."
4. "Another week of external calls. The highlights, such as they are."
5. "Cost check for the week ending {date}. Read time: under a minute."
6. "Sunday report. The short version: {one_line_summary}."

---

## Section headings

Consistent across all digests. Haiku drafts the prose glue between them.

1. **"The number"** — MTD spend + projection vs thresholds
2. **"Tier check"** — per-tier health summary (one line per tier)
3. **"Top jobs"** — top 5 by spend this week, with trend direction
4. **"Anomalies"** — any that fired this week (or "none" — keep the section, one-liner)
5. **"Band tuning"** — any bands adjusted this week (or omit section if none)

---

## Closer pool

Final line of the digest. Dry. Never a CTA.

1. "That's the week. The observatory is still watching."
2. "End of report. See you next Sunday."
3. "Numbers don't lie. They do occasionally drift, though."
4. "Next digest lands same time next week unless you turn it off."
