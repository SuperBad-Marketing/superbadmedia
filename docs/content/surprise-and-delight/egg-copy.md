# Surprise & Delight — Egg Copy

Content source for S&D spec Layer 2 (hidden eggs). Final copy for all 18 eggs (12 public + 6 admin).

All public eggs use bartender voice. All admin eggs use roommate voice. Copy is generated by `generateInVoice()` at surface-build time and cached — these are the seed/reference lines that the LLM uses as tone calibration.

---

## Public eggs (12)

### 1. Late-night visitor
**Trigger:** Visitor local time 02:00–05:00.
**Copy pool:**
- "it's {hour} where you are. genuinely, go to bed."
- "you're browsing marketing agencies at {hour}. we're concerned."
- "it's the middle of the night. this can wait."

### 2. Sunday researcher
**Trigger:** Sunday + search engine referrer + dwell > 45s.
**Copy pool (one-shot):**
- "sunday. you're researching marketing agencies on a sunday. we're a bit worried about you."
- "it's sunday and you're here. that's either dedication or a cry for help."

### 3. Melbourne public holiday (site closure)
**Trigger:** Date matches `/data/au-holidays.json`.
**Copy:** "australian public holiday. we're not working. neither should you."
*(Single line — this is a structural egg, not a rotation pool.)*

### 4. Fifth-time visitor
**Trigger:** 5th distinct calendar-day visit.
**Copy pool (one-shot):**
- "you keep coming back. if you'd like to actually talk to someone, andy@superbadmedia.com.au"
- "fifth visit. at this point you might as well say hello. andy@superbadmedia.com.au"

### 5. Returning visitor
**Trigger:** 2nd+ visit within 30 days (not 5th).
**Copy pool:**
- "you're back. we didn't do anything with the place."
- "welcome back. nothing's changed. or maybe everything has. hard to tell."
- "oh, you again."

### 6. LinkedIn referrer
**Trigger:** `document.referrer` matches LinkedIn URL.
**Copy pool:**
- "LinkedIn sent you here. we are, in a sense, sorry."
- "you came from LinkedIn. our condolences."
- "LinkedIn. bold of you to click through."

### 7. Google intent — cheap/discount
**Trigger:** Referrer query contains cheap/discount/affordable/low cost/budget/free.
**Copy pool (one-shot):**
- "we're not cheap. we're sorry. there's a door over there."
- "you searched for cheap. we are not that."
- "budget marketing agencies exist. we're not one of them."

### 8. Rapid scroller
**Trigger:** Top-to-bottom scroll in under 6 seconds.
**Copy:** "you scrolled past it in {seconds} seconds. here's the short version: {page_summary}."
*(Per-page summary is generated at build time. See per-page-configs.md.)*

### 9. Deep reader
**Trigger:** Dwell 4min+ on a single page, scroll depth > 70%.
**Copy:** "you actually read it. that's rare. here's the long version: {deeper_link}."
*(Per-page link target configured in per-page-configs.md. Fails closed if no target exists.)*

### 10. Abandoned tab
**Trigger:** Tab backgrounded 10+ minutes, then foregrounded.
**Copy pool:**
- "you opened us in a new tab and walked away. it's fine. we'll wait."
- "you left us open for {minutes} minutes. we kept the lights on."
- "back? we didn't move."

### 11. Melbourne rain
**Trigger:** Visitor in Australia/Melbourne timezone + Open-Meteo returns active precipitation.
**Copy pool:**
- "raining in Melbourne. we're glad you're inside."
- "it's raining. typical. at least you're here."
- "Melbourne weather. you know how it is."
*(Rain ambient sound fades in at 20% volume, respects `soundsEnabled` + `prefers-reduced-motion`.)*

### 12. Public CRT turn-off
**Trigger:** Visitor local time 01:00–04:59 Melbourne + 3min on site + no late-night egg this session.
**Copy:** "we've closed for the night. go to bed. the site will reopen at 7am melbourne time, whether you like it or not."
*(Single line — structural closure egg.)*

### Welcome egg (safety net)
**Trigger:** Session ends with no other egg having fired.
**Copy pool:**
- "you came, you saw, nothing happened. we noticed."
- "you visited. we clocked it. that's all."
- "first visit noted. nothing else to report."

## Admin eggs (6)

### 1. CRT turn-off
**Trigger:** Signed in after 01:30 Melbourne local on 3 of the last 7 days.
**Copy:** "you've been up until 2am three nights running. I'm pulling the plug."
**Below:** "close this tab."
*(CRT effect plays. No "never mind" button. 30-day cooldown.)*

### 2. Milestone spotter
**Trigger:** Client Context Engine detects an upcoming personal/business milestone in notes.
**Copy pool (notification):**
- "you wrote '{milestone_quote}' in the {contact_name} notes {timeframe} ago. want to send something?"
- "{contact_name} — {milestone_description} coming up on {date}. spotted it in the notes."

**Copy pool (drafted email):** Claude-generated, warm, brief, personal. Reads Brand DNA + contact context. Passes drift check. Example tone:
- "Happy birthday to {name} — {age} is a big one. Hope the cake situation is under control."
- "Congrats on the anniversary. {years} years is no small thing."

**SMS alternative:** shorter, same tone. Example:
- "Happy birthday {name}. {age} — big number. Cheers, Andy"

### 3. Three Wons in a session
**Trigger:** 3 deals finalised to Won in a single session.
**Copy:** "That's three. Either you're crushing it or it's a slow Tuesday."
*(30-day cooldown.)*

### 4. Weekend warrior
**Trigger:** Saturday or Sunday + 3+ admin session pings (~2h activity).
**Copy pool:**
- "It's the weekend. Log off. The leads will still be there Monday."
- "Saturday and you're still here. Admirable. Also, stop."

### 5. Inbox zero
**Trigger:** Zero pending scheduled tasks.
**Copy pool:**
- "Nothing pending. Either you're efficient or something's broken."
- "Task list: empty. That's either very good or very bad."

### 6. First client won
**Trigger:** Exactly 1 deal in Won stage (first ever). One-shot.
**Copy:** "First one. Remember this feeling — it gets quieter from here."
