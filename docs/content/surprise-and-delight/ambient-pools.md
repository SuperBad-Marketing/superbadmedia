# Surprise & Delight — Ambient Copy Seed Pools

Content source for S&D spec Layer 1 (ambient voice). Six surface categories per the closed list. Each pool provides seed content for `generateInVoice()` — these are the initial cached lines. The LLM regenerates on demand when Andy wants fresh ones.

All lines are SuperBad's own voice: dry, observational, self-deprecating, Melbourne. Never helpful. Never apologetic. Never explains the joke.

---

## 1. Empty states

Used when a screen has no data. The line is the whole content of the empty area.

### Pipeline / deals empty
- "no deals yet. either that's a problem or you just got here."
- "the pipeline is empty. it's not judging you."
- "nothing in the pipeline. the quiet before the everything."

### Prospects empty
- "no prospects yet. that's either a problem or a really good week."
- "zero prospects. the outreach engine is ready when you are."
- "the prospect list is empty. uncluttered, you might say."

### Inbox empty
- "nothing in the inbox. suspicious, but we'll take it."
- "inbox zero. try not to celebrate too loudly."
- "no messages. the internet is still working, apparently."

### Tasks empty
- "nothing pending. either you're efficient or something's broken."
- "no tasks. well done, or possibly a bug."
- "task list empty. enjoy this while it lasts."

### Content queue empty
- "no content queued. the engine has opinions about that."
- "nothing in the content pipeline. it's waiting politely."
- "empty queue. the newsletter writes itself when there's material."

### Client notes empty
- "no notes yet. the context engine needs something to work with."
- "notes: none. the more you write, the smarter this gets."
- "blank slate. start typing and the engine picks it up."

### General / fallback
- "nothing here yet."
- "empty. not broken, just early."
- "this page has nothing to show. give it time."

## 2. Error pages

### 404
- "nothing here. you might have typed something wrong, or we did."
- "page not found. it's not hiding — it genuinely doesn't exist."
- "404. the page you're looking for has either moved or never existed."
- "there's nothing at this address. could be worse."

### 500
- "something broke. we know. we're probably already looking at it."
- "internal error. the platform had a moment."
- "that didn't work. the details are being logged."
- "broken. not ideal. the fix is in progress or about to be."

### Generic error
- "that didn't go as planned."
- "something went sideways. try again or wait a minute."

## 3. Loading copy

Short. Under 10 words. Never apologetic.

- "pulling the numbers."
- "one moment."
- "getting that for you."
- "loading. it's not stalling, just thinking."
- "almost there."
- "aggregating."
- "working on it."
- "hang on."
- "fetching."
- "crunching."

## 4. Success toasts

Confirmation after a successful action. Understated. Never celebratory.

### General
- "done."
- "saved."
- "that worked."
- "noted."
- "updated."

### Specific actions
- "email sent."
- "quote saved. they'll get it when you're ready."
- "invoice created."
- "prospect added."
- "deal moved."
- "settings updated."
- "task completed."
- "published."
- "archived. it's not gone, just filed."
- "deleted. no takebacks on that one."

## 5. Placeholder text

Input field and search bar placeholders. Observational, never instructional.

### Search bars
- "search for something."
- "type a name, a number, a hunch."
- "looking for something specific?"
- "search."

### Text inputs
- "start typing."
- "say something."
- "this is where the words go."

### Notes / freeform fields
- "add context."
- "write something useful. or not."
- "notes go here."

### Filter / empty filter states
- "no results for that. try being less specific."
- "nothing matches. broaden the search."
- "filtered to nothing. that's thorough."

## 6. Morning brief narrative

Opening line for the Daily Cockpit brief. Sets the tone without forced optimism. The Opus brief generator fills the rest — this is just the hook.

### Calm day
- "quiet morning. here's what's on."
- "not much to report. the kind of day you don't notice."
- "light agenda. let's keep it that way."

### Busy day
- "full day. here's the summary."
- "a lot on today. starting with the urgent bits."
- "busy one. coffee first, then this."

### Mixed signals
- "some good, some less good. starting with the numbers."
- "a couple of things worth looking at. nothing on fire."
- "mostly fine. one or two things flagged."

### Monday
- "monday. the pipeline doesn't take weekends off."
- "start of the week. here's where things stand."

### Friday
- "friday. wrap-up edition."
- "end of the week. the highlights, such as they are."
