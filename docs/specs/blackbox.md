# SuperBad BlackBox — Product Spec

**Brainstorm output. Locked 2026-04-22.**

SuperBad's flagship SaaS product: a fully automated marketing platform that solves the entire growth spiral — invisible online presence, stale website, dead social channels, leaking leads, and thin top-of-funnel — in one subscription. The name says it: marketing goes in, results come out, the subscriber doesn't need to understand the middle.

BlackBox is built on top of existing Lite infrastructure: Content Engine (`content-engine.md`), Lead Generation (`lead-generation.md`), Brand DNA Assessment (`brand-dna-assessment.md`), SaaS Subscription Billing (`saas-subscription-billing.md`), and the subscriber portal. It composes these systems into a single product experience.

---

## 1. The problem (one spiral, not five)

Most growing businesses experience these problems simultaneously:

1. **Can't stay visible on social** — posting dies, guilt builds, brand goes quiet.
2. **Leads leak** — enquiries come in, follow-up is too slow or nonexistent.
3. **No idea what's working** — spending on marketing but flying blind.
4. **Website is stale and embarrassing** — can't justify a rebuild, so it rots.
5. **Not enough leads coming in** — the top of the funnel is thin.

These aren't five separate problems. They reinforce each other. A business with a stale website can't generate leads. The few leads that arrive leak because follow-up is manual. Social is dead because nobody has time. They can't tell what's working because nothing is. BlackBox breaks the spiral by solving everything at once.

## 2. Product structure

One platform, three tiers. Differentiated by capability, not volume. Content output is consistent across all tiers.

### 2.1 Content output (all tiers)

- **1 blog post per week** — keyword-driven, SEO-focused, generated by the Content Engine.
- **Up to 5 social posts per week** — repurposed from blog content, with variations for different placements and formats (feed posts, stories, LinkedIn, etc.).
- **Content flow:** keyword research → blog post → social repurposed from blog. Blog is the SEO asset; social is the distribution layer.

**Entry** solves "we're invisible and embarrassed."

### 2.3 Popcorn pricing logic

Entry captures them. Growth is the obvious choice — outreach and lead gen are why most businesses are here. Scale is a $300 jump from Growth for managed paid ads, the classic small-jump-big-upgrade.

### 2.4 Future fourth tier

Reserved for high-volume subscribers who need more content output (more posts, more blogs). Not specced yet — demand will shape it.

## 5. Living website

### 5.1 Architecture

Templated but personalised. A set of high-quality templates populated with the subscriber's Brand DNA — colours, voice, copy, imagery. Looks custom to the subscriber; the structure is standardised under the hood for maintainability and conversion optimisation at scale.

**Standard page set:** Home, About, Services, Contact.

### 5.2 "Living" behaviour

The website is a service, not a deliverable. The platform continuously updates it:

- New blog posts published automatically from the Content Engine.
- Testimonials/reviews pulled in as new reviews arrive.
- Service pages refreshed seasonally.
- SEO tweaked based on performance data.
- The site is always current without the subscriber touching it.

### 5.3 Hosting

- **Subdomain fallback:** subscribers without a domain (or not ready to switch) start on `theirbusiness.superbadmedia.com.au`. Live in hours, zero friction.
- **Custom domain:** subscribers delegate DNS or point their domain to BlackBox hosting. Setup wizard handles configuration.
- **Upgrade path:** subdomain → custom domain at any time.

Entry tier subscribers may start on subdomain. Growth and Scale subscribers are guided to custom domain during onboarding.

## 6. Content engine integration

BlackBox uses the Content Engine (`content-engine.md`) for all blog and social content generation. The content hierarchy:

1. **Keyword research** — platform identifies high-value search terms for the subscriber's market.
2. **Blog posts** — AI writes long-form content targeting those keywords, published to the living website.
3. **Social content** — repurposed from blog posts, cut into platform-native formats per channel (feed, stories, LinkedIn, etc.).
4. **SEO compounds** — every post builds domain authority, every social share drives backlinks and traffic.

## 7. Outreach engine (Growth + Scale)

BlackBox uses the Lead Generation system (`lead-generation.md`) — the same ICP scoring, prospect qualification, and personalised outreach engine built for SuperBad's own lead gen — pointed at the subscriber's market.

### 7.1 Sending infrastructure

Dedicated sending domain per subscriber (e.g. `updates-theirbusiness.com`). Platform controls and warms the domain. Protects the subscriber's primary domain reputation. Recipient sees the subscriber's business name, not SuperBad's.

### 7.2 Outreach volume

Market-calibrated. During onboarding, BlackBox analyses the subscriber's ICP and geography, estimates the addressable prospect pool, and recommends a sustainable monthly volume. Platform manages pacing to protect deliverability. No fixed number in marketing — "as many qualified prospects as your market supports."

### 7.3 Lead follow-up

AI first response with subscriber override. Every inbound enquiry gets an instant, personalised, brand-voice reply. Acknowledges the question, provides relevant info, invites booking or continued conversation. Subscriber is notified and can take over at any time. If they don't, the platform follows up. Leads never go cold.

The AI chat can action lead management with confirmation (see §8.2).

## 8. Subscriber portal experience

### 8.1 Home screen

AI chat — same chat-first portal layout as retainer clients. The chat is the primary interface. Conversational, brand-voiced, knows everything about the subscriber's account. Examples:

- "How's my outreach going?"
- "What keywords am I ranking for?"
- "Show me this week's posts before they go out."
- "Pause my outreach this week."

### 8.2 AI chat capabilities

Full control with confirmation. The chat can read and write — answer questions, show stats, and take actions. Any action that changes something requires one-click confirmation before executing.

### 8.3 Menu

All other surfaces branch off the menu: content calendar, lead pipeline, website preview, performance stats, outreach activity, settings. Available when the subscriber wants to dig in, never pushed.

### 8.4 Notifications

Pings for things that matter: new lead, review to approve, milestone hit, content ready for review (if approval mode requires it).

### 8.5 Weekly digest

"Here's what BlackBox did this week." Posts published, leads generated, site visits, reviews handled. Lands in their inbox. The subscriber feels the machine running even if they never log in.

## 9. Content approval

Subscriber chooses their preference in settings during onboarding (changeable anytime):

| Mode | Behaviour |
|------|-----------|
| **Auto-publish** | Everything publishes on schedule. True BlackBox. |
| **Approve-first** | Everything queues for approval before publishing. Subscriber reviews and approves each piece. |
| **Auto with override window** | Content scheduled with a 24-hour preview window. Review and tweak if desired; ignore and it publishes automatically. |

## 10. Paid ads management (Scale only)

### 10.1 What BlackBox manages

Campaign creation, ad creative generation, audience targeting, bid optimisation, performance monitoring, creative refresh. All automated.

### 10.2 Ad spend

Subscriber pays ad spend directly to Meta/Google. BlackBox manages the campaigns; the $897/mo is purely management. No cash flow risk, no ad spend pass-through.

### 10.3 Budget guidance

Soft minimum with recommendation. During onboarding, BlackBox recommends a budget based on the subscriber's market and goals. No hard block. The AI chat nudges if budget is too thin: "At your current spend, expect X leads per month. Increasing to $Y would roughly double that."

## 11. Onboarding

Wizard-led, step-by-step, per the "setup is hand-held, never self-served" rule. Pre-populated where possible — platform fills gaps with its own research.

### 11.1 Data collected

| Step | What | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Brand DNA | Voice, values, audience, visual identity | Same questionnaire as retainer clients. Cold outreach prospects get pre-seeded data. |
| ICP definition | Ideal customer, industry, size, geography | For Growth/Scale outreach targeting. |
| Keyword seeds | What they want to be found for | Auto-derived, subscriber approves/tweaks. |
| Business basics | Services, pricing, location(s), hours, team | Populates website and content. |
| Existing assets | Logo, photos, existing content | Carried into website and social. |
| Access credentials | Social accounts, Google Business Profile, domain DNS | Connect existing accounts or create new ones. |
| Content approval preference | Auto / approve-first / auto with override | See §9. |

### 11.2 Social accounts

If the subscriber has existing social accounts, they connect them. If they don't, BlackBox creates new profiles as part of onboarding, branded using their Brand DNA. The wizard handles both paths.

### 11.3 Time to first value

Social content starts flowing within hours. Website builds in parallel, live within the first week. By the time the site is live, posts are already going out and the machine feels like it's running. Outreach (Growth/Scale) layers on once the sending domain is warmed.

## 14. What BlackBox is NOT

- **Not a dashboard product.** Subscribers don't need to learn a tool. The AI chat is the interface; the dashboard is a reference.
- **Not a template marketplace.** Websites are personalised from Brand DNA, not picked from a catalogue.
- **Not a DIY tool.** Subscribers don't write copy, choose keywords, or manage campaigns. BlackBox does the work.
- **Not Content Studio.** Content Studio is a separate standalone product. BlackBox's content pipeline is native to Lite via the Content Engine.
- **Not "SuperBad Lite" to subscribers.** Client-facing surfaces say "SuperBad BlackBox." Lite is the internal/admin name only.
